VIVATLAS Clipper privacy policy

The clipper talks to one place only: the VIVATLAS server whose address you enter. It is a server you run yourself or one you have an account on. The clipper has no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services, and its author receives nothing from it.

What it sends to your server, and when:
- When you press Add to VIVATLAS: the address and title of the current page (or the link you typed), the readable text of that page, and whether the card should be private or public.
- When you sign in: your email, your password and, if your account uses one, a two-step code. The password is not stored.
- On every request: your session token, so the server knows it is you.

What it keeps in your browser:
- The server address, your session token, your email and display name, and your default for new cards (private or public), in the extension's local storage. Signing out removes the token.
- It reads, and when you sign in through the clipper sets, the session cookie of your VIVATLAS server, so the website and the clipper stay signed in as the same person.

What it does not do:
- It does not read pages in the background. It reads the current tab only when you open the clipper on it, and sends it only when you press Add to VIVATLAS.
- It cannot see any site other than your server and the tab you clip.

What happens to the data on the server is up to whoever runs that server. VIVATLAS stores a clip as a card in the space you chose: private (only you) or public (everyone on that server).
